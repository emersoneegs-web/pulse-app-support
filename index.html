📱 About Pulse

Pulse is a simple and intuitive heart rate monitoring application that uses your device's camera and flash to measure your heart rate. Perfect for quick health checks and fitness tracking.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer:

This app is designed for general wellness and fitness purposes only. It is NOT a medical device and should not be used for diagnosing or treating any medical condition. Always consult with a healthcare professional for medical advice.

🔍 How to Use

  • Open the App

    Launch Pulse on your device

  • Position Your Finger

    Place your index finger gently over the rear camera lens

  • Cover the Flash

    Make sure your finger also covers the camera flash

  • Stay Still

    Keep your finger steady and don't press too hard

  • Wait for Measurement

    The app will automatically detect your heart rate

  • View Results

    Your heart rate will be displayed in beats per minute (BPM)

  • Save to History

    Optionally save your measurement to track over time

  • Repeat as Needed

    Take multiple measurements throughout the day

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How accurate is this app?
A: The app provides reasonably accurate readings for general wellness purposes. However, for medical-grade accuracy, please use certified medical devices.
Q: Why isn't the measurement working?
A: Make sure you're covering both the camera and flash completely with your finger. Ensure your finger isn't pressed too hard and you're in a well-lit environment.
Q: What's a normal heart rate?
A: For adults, a normal resting heart rate ranges from 60-100 BPM. Athletes may have lower rates. Consult your doctor if you have concerns.
Q: Can I use this app for medical diagnosis?
A: No. This app is for informational and wellness purposes only. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice and diagnosis.
Q: Does the app store my data?
A: Your measurement history is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your health data to external servers.
Q: Which devices are compatible?
A: Pulse works on most modern smartphones with a rear camera and flash. Performance may vary depending on your device's camera quality.
