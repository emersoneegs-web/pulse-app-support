Pulse

ACLS Training & Code Simulation Support

About Pulse

Pulse is a training app designed to help healthcare professionals practice ACLS (Advanced Cardiac Life Support) scenarios. It provides timing tools, reference materials, and simulation aids for training purposes only.

Important: This app is for educational training only. It is not intended for use in actual medical emergencies or patient care.

Features

  • Code Timer: Track elapsed time during practice scenarios
  • 2-Minute CPR Cycles: Automatic alerts for compression/rhythm check intervals
  • Drug Administration Buttons: Log common ACLS medications with timestamps
  • Metronome: Adjustable from 80-140 BPM for compression rate guidance
  • ACLS Algorithms: Quick reference guides for common scenarios
  • Session History: Review past training sessions
  • Offline Operation: Works without internet connection

How to Use

  1. Open the app and tap "Start Code" to begin a practice session
  2. The timer will automatically count up from zero
  3. Use the drug buttons to log medication administration during practice
  4. The app will alert you every 2 minutes for rhythm checks
  5. Enable the metronome if you want compression rate guidance
  6. Tap "End Code" when finishing your training scenario
  7. Review the session summary and export if needed
  8. Access algorithm references from the menu for quick study

FAQ

Q: Is this app medically certified?
A: No. This is an educational training tool only. It is not certified for use in actual medical emergencies.
Q: Does the app collect my data?
A: All session data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Q: Can I use this during a real emergency?
A: No. This app is designed for training only. In real emergencies, follow your institution's protocols and contact emergency services immediately.
Q: Are the algorithms current?
A: The algorithms are based on published ACLS guidelines. However, always verify with current official sources and your institution's protocols.
Q: Why do the timers sometimes drift?
A: Mobile devices may pause background processes. For best accuracy, keep the app in the foreground during training sessions.
Q: Can I export my training logs?
A: Yes. From the History screen, you can export session data as text or share it with your study group.
