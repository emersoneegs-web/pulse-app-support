Privacy Policy - Pulse

Last Updated: October 24, 2025

Overview

Pulse is a CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation) training application designed to help users learn and practice life-saving techniques. This privacy policy explains our commitment to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

ZERO DATA COLLECTION

Pulse is built with privacy as a core principle. We collect absolutely no data:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No data is transmitted to our servers
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No Protected Health Information (PHI) is collected or stored
  • No device access beyond basic functionality required for the app to operate
  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies

Local Storage

All data created within the app stays exclusively on your device. Any training progress, settings, or information you input remains local and is never transmitted externally.

Permissions

Pulse requests NO special permissions beyond what is necessary for basic app functionality. We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device features.

Third-Party Services

Pulse does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The app operates entirely offline and independently.

Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data whatsoever, Pulse is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. No personal information is collected from anyone, regardless of age.

Data Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your information. Your training sessions and any data you create remain securely on your device.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We will maintain our commitment to zero data collection.
