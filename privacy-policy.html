Privacy Policy - Pulse

Last Updated: October 24, 2025

Overview

Pulse is a CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation) training tool designed to help users learn and practice proper CPR techniques through interactive guidance and metronome-based timing. Your privacy is our top priority.

Data Collection

ZERO DATA COLLECTION

Pulse collects absolutely NO data from users. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • No Data Transmission: No data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use analytics tools, tracking pixels, or any monitoring software.
  • No Protected Health Information (PHI): We do not collect, store, or process any health-related data.
  • No Device Access: The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device features beyond what is absolutely necessary for core functionality.
  • No User Accounts: No registration, login, or account creation is required.
  • No Cookies: We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Local Storage

All training data, preferences, and app settings remain exclusively on your device. This data is:

  • Stored locally using your device's storage
  • Never transmitted over the internet
  • Completely under your control
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app

Permissions

Pulse requests NO permissions beyond the basic functionality required to run the app. We do not request access to:

  • Camera or photos
  • Microphone
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Phone or messaging
  • Network access (beyond basic app functionality)

Third-Party Services

Pulse does NOT integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage services
  • Payment processors

Children's Privacy

Because Pulse collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breaches related to your personal information. All training data remains securely on your device, protected by your device's own security measures.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
